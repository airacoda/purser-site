Purser Money

One calm view of your money.

Purser is a money app for iPhone and iPad. It brings every account, card, loan and bill into one place and shows what is safe to spend until payday, what is due before then, whether the bills account covers it, and how savings are tracking.

What it does

  • Safe to spend, worked out from how you really pay your cards, not from a checking balance.
  • Spending by category and merchant, with simple rules for filing new purchases.
  • Recurring bills and subscriptions, with a warning before an account runs short.
  • Accounts, loans and property together, with balances over time.
  • Penny, a built-in advisor who answers questions about your own numbers.

What it never does

Purser never moves money. It reads and advises; every payment stays in the bank's own app. There is no tracking and no analytics.

Purser is in private preview. For now it is shared with invited households. Wider availability is planned for later.

Questions go to support.
