Purser Money is made and supported by Erick Ingleby. For help, questions about your data, or to have your data deleted, write to erick@ingleby.com. Replies come within two days.

Common questions

Can I get the app?

Purser is in private preview. For now it is shared privately with invited households, through Sign in with Apple, and is not yet on the public App Store. Wider availability is planned for later.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, then Delete account. Purser asks you to confirm with Sign in with Apple, revokes its Sign in with Apple access, and permanently deletes your data on its server. If you are the last member of the household, the household's data and the link to the bank feed are deleted too. The details are in the privacy policy. You can also write to erick@ingleby.com.

Can Purser move my money?

No. It reads and advises. Payments and transfers stay in your bank's own app.

How do I stop sharing my Apple Card?

On the iPhone, open Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Wallet, and switch Purser off. Purser stops receiving Apple Card data at once.

How do I delete my data?

Write to the address above. Your data is removed from the service and from your devices.
