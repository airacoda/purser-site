Common questions

Can I get the app?

Purser is distributed privately, by invitation, to the members of one household. It is not on the public App Store.

Can Purser move my money?

No. It reads and advises. Payments and transfers stay in your bank's own app.

How do I stop sharing my Apple Card?

On the iPhone, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Wallet, and switch Purser off. Purser stops receiving Apple Card data at once.
